Complete **AI development services** cover the system around a model. Buyers should expect discovery, data work, evaluation, product integration, safety controls, deployment, and operational improvement.

## Discovery and baseline

Define the user, task, current process, input, desired output, acceptable error, human responsibility, and measurable result. Compare AI with simpler alternatives. Identify legal, privacy, security, and brand constraints with appropriate specialists.

## Data preparation

Scope access, permissions, cleaning, labeling, document processing, metadata, freshness, retention, and deletion. Establish test data that represents real variation and important edge cases.

## Model selection and orchestration

Evaluate candidate models against the task rather than public benchmarks alone. Include prompts, structured output, retrieval, tools, routing, fallback, caching, latency, and cost.

## Experience and integration

Design uncertainty, citations, editing, approval, escalation, and feedback into the interface. Connect approved systems with least privilege and server-side authorization. Validate outputs before writing to business records.

## Evaluation and controls

Build automated scenario tests and human review for important qualities. Add input and output controls, tool limits, audit trails, rate and spend limits, and incident procedures.

## Production operations

Monitor task success, failure type, escalation, latency, availability, token or inference use, cost, and business outcome. Version model, prompt, retrieval, tool, and evaluation changes.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is a prototype part of AI development services?

Yes. A prototype should test the riskiest feasibility or experience assumption with representative data.

### What should not be omitted?

Evaluation, authorization, failure handling, monitoring, data controls, and human responsibility.

### How is ongoing support different?

AI systems require model and provider review, evaluation updates, knowledge freshness, cost monitoring, incident response, and workflow improvement.
