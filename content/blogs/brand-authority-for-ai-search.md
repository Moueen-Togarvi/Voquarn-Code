**Brand authority for AI search** is less about domain metrics than about corroboration: whether what you say about yourself is confirmed by sources you do not control. Generative systems weigh agreement across independent sources, so a claim confined to your own domain reads as unverified.

This is the slowest part of AI visibility work and the hardest for a competitor to copy, which makes it the part worth starting first.

## Why corroboration drives selection

When a model composes an answer, it is effectively deciding which assertions are safe to repeat. Assertions supported by several independent sources are safe. Assertions found in one place, especially one with a commercial interest, are not.

This explains a pattern many teams find confusing: a well-written, technically sound page that still never gets cited. The page is fine. The claim has no external support.

## Entity clarity comes first

Before authority can accrue, the system must resolve you as a distinct entity. That requires consistency.

Use one company name form everywhere. Use the same short description across your site, directory listings, social profiles, and publication bios. Keep location, service vocabulary, and founding details identical across sources.

Inconsistency here is common and costly. A model that cannot confidently determine whether two mentions refer to the same organization tends to cite neither.

## Which sources carry weight

Not all mentions are equal. In rough order of value for corroboration:

- **Established industry publications** in your sector, especially where you contribute substantive analysis rather than promotional copy.
- **Technical documentation and reference material** that other people cite, including open source projects, specifications, and integration guides.
- **Recognized directories and review platforms** for your category, kept accurate and current.
- **Academic, standards, or government sources** where your work is genuinely relevant.
- **Substantive community participation** in the forums where your buyers ask questions, where answers are detailed enough to be quoted.
- **Conference and podcast appearances** with transcripts or written summaries that are indexable.

Low-value: syndicated press releases, paid listing farms, and guest posts on sites that exist to sell links. These add noise without adding trust.

## Unlinked mentions matter more than they used to

Classic link building valued the hyperlink. For corroboration, the mention itself carries the signal, because the model reads the text rather than following a graph.

That widens the useful target set to podcast transcripts, panel summaries, community answers, and coverage that names you without linking. It also means digital PR aimed at accurate description is more valuable than PR aimed purely at link acquisition.

## Sequencing the work

Start with accuracy: audit existing third-party profiles and correct outdated descriptions, wrong services, and stale contact details. This is fast and prevents misdescription in answers.

Then close the gap list from your visibility audit, meaning the sources cited when competitors appear and you do not. Those sources are demonstrably trusted in your category.

Then build ongoing contribution: a regular cadence of substantive publication, documentation, and participation. Treat it as a standing program rather than a campaign, because the benefit compounds and decays slowly.

## Measuring it

Track description accuracy in sampled assistant answers, the count of independent sources confirming your priority claims, and citation rate over time. Do not expect an isolated metric; authority shows up as steadier, more accurate presence across many prompts rather than a single jump.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long does this take?

Accuracy fixes show within weeks. Genuine corroboration typically takes one to two quarters to influence answers, and continues compounding after that.

### Do we still need backlinks?

Yes for conventional search, where links continue to pass authority. For AI answers the mention carries most of the value, so pursue both.

### Can we buy our way into this?

Paid placement on low-quality sites adds nothing. Legitimate sponsorship and advertising do not substitute for independent confirmation.

### What if a source describes us wrongly?

Contact the publisher with a correction, and strengthen the accurate version elsewhere so the weight of evidence shifts.
