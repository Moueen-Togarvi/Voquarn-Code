Complete **Professional website development services** combine business strategy, content, design, engineering, search foundations, and operation. Use this checklist to compare proposals that may appear similar while including very different work.

## Strategy and structure

- Priority audiences and conversion goals.
- Competitor and current-site evidence.
- Sitemap and page purposes.
- Content inventory, ownership, and approvals.
- Measurement plan and baseline.

## Content and experience

Require message hierarchy, real content, calls to action, proof, responsive layouts, navigation, forms, errors, and accessible interaction. Confirm who writes, edits, migrates, and approves copy.

## Technical implementation

The scope should name platform, CMS, hosting, integrations, browser support, security, performance targets, and maintainability. Ask why the stack fits editors and long-term ownership.

## Search foundations

Include descriptive URLs, titles, descriptions, headings, internal links, crawlable navigation, canonical handling, sitemap, redirects, robots controls, and relevant structured data. Search performance also requires useful content and ongoing authority.

## Quality and launch

Test mobile and desktop, keyboard access, contrast, forms, analytics, metadata, links, redirects, performance, security headers, error pages, and backup. Define DNS, certificates, rollout, monitoring, and rollback.

## Ownership and support

Client ownership should include domain, hosting, repository, CMS, analytics, media, and third-party accounts. Separate launch warranty from ongoing care and content improvement.

## Define evidence at handover

Request a launch report containing tested browsers and devices, accessibility checks, performance results, redirects, metadata, analytics verification, form destinations, backups, account ownership, and known limitations. This gives the client a baseline and prevents handover from becoming a verbal assurance that everything was checked.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many pages should a business website have?

Enough to answer distinct audience needs and search intents clearly. Page count should follow information architecture, not a package number.

### Is SEO included automatically?

Technical foundations should be. Keyword research, content production, link earning, and ongoing optimization need explicit scope.

### What is the strongest acceptance test?

Representative users can find information and complete priority actions across supported devices, with analytics and production checks working.
