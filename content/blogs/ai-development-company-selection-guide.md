An **AI development company** should be evaluated on production evidence rather than impressive demonstrations. Buyers need a team capable of saying when AI is unsuitable, designing representative evaluations, and integrating models into dependable software.

## Test use-case judgment

Describe a business problem and ask the company to identify assumptions, baseline alternatives, data needs, failure consequences, and a small test. Strong teams compare rules, search, workflow automation, and AI rather than forcing one solution.

## Review data practice

Ask how data will be accessed, cleaned, permissioned, retained, and deleted. For knowledge systems, inspect citation and access control. For predictive systems, inspect labels, leakage, segment performance, and drift.

## Demand evaluation detail

The proposal should define a test set, metrics, important failure categories, thresholds, and review. “Users will test it” is not sufficient. Evaluation should be repeatable across model and prompt changes.

## Inspect product engineering

AI products still need UX, APIs, databases, identity, integrations, queues, monitoring, security, and support. Meet engineers responsible for these layers. Ask how the system handles provider outage, malformed output, latency, and cost spikes.

## Clarify commercial ownership

Confirm ownership of code, prompts, evaluation sets, pipelines, indexes, accounts, telemetry, and feedback data. Understand model-provider fees and how usage will be controlled.

## Compare commercial assumptions

Normalize provider fees, model usage, data preparation, expert review, cloud resources, evaluation maintenance, and support. Ask how cost changes at ten times the pilot volume and what limits prevent unexpected spend. A low build estimate can hide a production model whose unit economics do not support the intended business case.

## Frequently asked questions

### What proof should an AI company provide?

Relevant production examples, evaluation artifacts, failure analysis, architecture decisions, and references able to discuss post-launch operation.

### Should it promise a specific accuracy?

Only for a defined dataset, task, metric, and conditions. Broad accuracy claims are not meaningful.

### What is a good first engagement?

A paid discovery and feasibility pilot with representative data, explicit controls, and a proceed-or-stop decision.
