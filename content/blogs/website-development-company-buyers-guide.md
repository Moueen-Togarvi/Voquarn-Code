A **Website development company** should build a discoverable, credible, measurable route from visitor need to business action. Buyers should evaluate strategy, content, and post-launch ownership alongside visual design.

## Start with audience and intent

Define priority visitors, their questions, evidence needs, objections, and next steps. Structure navigation and pages around those journeys. The company should challenge internal jargon and organizationally convenient menus.

## Plan content before layouts

Establish page purpose, message hierarchy, proof, calls to action, and content ownership. Real content reveals design and SEO needs that placeholder copy hides.

## Evaluate technical SEO

Review crawlable links, rendering, metadata, canonical URLs, sitemap, robots controls, redirects, structured data, semantic headings, and mobile performance. Avoid promises of guaranteed rankings.

## Inspect quality and accessibility

Test responsive behavior, forms, errors, keyboard navigation, focus, labels, contrast, images, browsers, speed, security headers, and analytics. Define a launch checklist.

## Confirm CMS and ownership

Editors should update routine content safely with previews and permissions. The client should own domain, hosting, code, analytics, media, and third-party accounts.

## Protect an existing site's equity

If replacing a live site, inventory valuable URLs, backlinks, organic landing pages, metadata, content, and conversions. Map old URLs to the closest relevant new destinations and test redirects before launch. Monitor indexing, crawl errors, traffic, and conversion after release rather than assuming visual improvement preserves search performance. Keep the old analytics baseline and investigate material changes by page and query.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I compare portfolios?

Inspect live sites on mobile, test speed and accessibility, and ask about business results, constraints, and maintenance.

### Should content writing be included?

Assign it explicitly. Strategy, drafting, editing, migration, and approval require owners and time.

### What happens after launch?

Monitor search, performance, conversion, errors, forms, and content. Maintain and improve based on evidence.
