The term **AI SEO agency** now covers two very different services, and conflating them causes most of the disappointment in this market.

One meaning is an agency that uses AI to produce search content faster. The other is an agency that makes you visible inside AI-generated answers. The first is a production method. The second is a discipline. Establish which one a provider means before discussing price.

## Production efficiency is not a strategy

Agencies in the first category use models to draft briefs, outlines, and articles, then publish at volume. The efficiency is real, and used well it frees expert time for research and editing.

Used badly, it produces large quantities of undifferentiated content that says what every other source says. That content rarely earns rankings, and it actively hurts AI search visibility, because generative engines select passages that add support rather than repeat consensus.

If a proposal is priced per article and scaled by volume, you are buying production. Ask what makes any individual page worth citing.

## Visibility inside AI answers is the harder service

The second category targets citation in assistants and AI overviews. The work is technical, editorial, and reputational at once: making content reachable by AI crawlers, structuring claims so they can be quoted, and building corroboration across sources you do not own.

This is slower and less packageable than article production, which is why fewer agencies do it well. It is also where the durable advantage sits, because corroboration compounds and is hard for a competitor to replicate quickly.

## What a competent engagement includes

- Technical foundations: server-rendered content, crawler access verified by user agent, clean canonicals, fast responses, accurate structured data.
- Conventional search performance, because it gates the candidate pool that AI answers draw from.
- A frozen prompt set and repeated sampling to measure citation rate, with method disclosed.
- Content restructured so key claims are self-contained and quotable, not only new articles.
- Original material a summary cannot replace: data, tools, comparisons, documented experience.
- Off-site corroboration work with named targets.

## How AI should be used in the workflow

Reasonable uses include research synthesis, outline drafting, identifying coverage gaps, generating structured data, clustering queries by intent, and first-pass editing. These accelerate experts.

Unreasonable uses are publishing unedited output, generating content on subjects nobody on the team understands, and mass-producing pages to occupy keyword space. The failure mode is not detection; it is that the content is not worth citing.

Ask any provider a direct question: who reviews the output, and what is their expertise in our subject? If the answer is vague, the model is doing the thinking.

## Judging results

Set expectations across three horizons. Technical fixes show within weeks. Conventional ranking movement takes one to two quarters on competitive terms. AI citation share moves on a similar or slower timeline, because it depends on third-party corroboration.

Report conversions and qualified enquiries alongside sessions. Traffic falling while enquiries hold is a normal outcome in an environment where summaries absorb informational queries.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is AI-written content penalized?

Search guidance focuses on quality and usefulness rather than production method. Low-value content performs badly whether a person or a model wrote it.

### Should we replace our SEO agency with an AI SEO agency?

Only if the new provider covers both conventional search and AI visibility. Dropping conventional SEO weakens the signals AI answers rely on.

### How do we verify a provider's claims?

Ask for the measurement method, the prompt set, and raw sampled answers. Providers doing real work can produce these readily.

### What should we do first?

Verify crawler access and rendering. It is cheap, fast, and blocks everything else when broken.
