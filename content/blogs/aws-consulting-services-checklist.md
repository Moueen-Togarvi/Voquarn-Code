Strong **AWS consulting services** connect Amazon Web Services expertise with your workload, team, risk, and economics. A large catalog of AWS services is not an architecture. Every component should solve a defined requirement and have an owner.

## Workload and business context

- Are users, traffic, latency, data, availability, recovery, and regions documented?
- Does the consultant understand current incidents and delivery constraints?
- Is the expected business result measurable?
- Are build, migrate, retain, and retire options considered?

## Account and identity foundation

Review multi-account structure, federated access, least privilege, service roles, emergency access, logging, and separation of production. Root credentials and long-lived keys require strict control.

## Network and data security

Ask how public exposure, segmentation, encryption, secrets, keys, data retention, backups, and audit evidence are managed. Security controls should be automated and monitored, not described only in a document.

## Reliability and recovery

Architecture should start from business recovery requirements. Review multi-AZ design, dependency failure, scaling, quotas, backups, restoration tests, incident response, and service objectives.

## Delivery and observability

Expect infrastructure as code, reviewed changes, environment consistency, deployment automation, metrics, logs, traces, alerts, and runbooks. Dashboards should reflect user-critical services rather than every available metric.

## Cost management

Require allocation tags or accounts, budgets, anomaly alerts, rightsizing, storage lifecycle, commitment strategy, and unit economics. Discounts cannot repair an inefficient workload.

## Provider evaluation

Certifications and AWS partner status are useful signals, not proof of fit. Review relevant artifacts, speak with references, meet the proposed team, and run a focused paid assessment.

## Frequently asked questions

### Should everything move to AWS?

No. Move workloads when the business case, technical fit, and operating model justify it.

### Is serverless always cheaper?

It can be economical for variable workloads, but cost depends on usage shape, design, and supporting services.

### What should consultants hand over?

Code, accounts, diagrams, decisions, policies, dashboards, runbooks, cost model, tests, and training.
