A **digital agency** usually focuses on brand, content, campaigns, websites, and audience growth. A **software agency** focuses on applications, platforms, integrations, and engineering systems. Choose according to the primary problem, or use a partner that can prove both capabilities.

## When should you hire a digital agency?

A digital agency fits when the goal is stronger positioning, creative production, campaign execution, organic visibility, paid acquisition, or website conversion. Its work typically interacts with marketing teams and customer-facing channels.

## When should you hire a software agency?

A software agency fits when the work requires complex workflows, user accounts, data models, APIs, automation, reliability, or long-term product engineering. It usually collaborates with product, operations, and technology owners.

## Where do the services overlap?

Websites, ecommerce, analytics, personalization, marketing automation, and customer portals sit between the two. In these projects, clarify who owns strategy, design, frontend code, backend systems, data, testing, deployment, and measurement.

## How do you evaluate a hybrid agency?

Ask for evidence in both domains and meet the specialists responsible for each. Confirm that marketing goals do not weaken security or maintainability, and that engineering choices do not ignore content and conversion needs.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is a web design agency a digital agency?

Often, but service definitions vary. Evaluate the actual team and scope rather than the label.

### Can two separate agencies work together?

Yes, if responsibilities, shared tools, decision rights, and integration points are clear.

### Which agency should lead a website project?

The lead should match the dominant risk. A brand-led site may be led by digital strategy; a portal or complex commerce build may need engineering leadership.
