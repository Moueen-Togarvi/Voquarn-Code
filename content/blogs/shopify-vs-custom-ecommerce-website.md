Choose **Shopify** when you want a proven hosted commerce platform, standard selling workflows, and faster launch. Choose a **custom ecommerce website** when unique operations, product logic, integrations, or customer experiences create enough value to justify greater engineering responsibility.

## Where does Shopify fit best?

Shopify provides managed hosting, a commerce admin, checkout, product and order tools, themes, and an app ecosystem. It suits many direct-to-consumer and small-to-mid-market stores, especially when the operating model follows familiar ecommerce patterns.

The tradeoff is working within platform rules, plan capabilities, app dependencies, and available extension points.

## When does custom ecommerce make sense?

Custom development may be justified for complex product configuration, unusual pricing, specialized marketplaces, deep operational workflows, regulated requirements, or experiences that standard platforms cannot support efficiently.

Custom does not mean every component must be invented. A sound architecture can combine established payment, search, tax, and content services with proprietary workflows.

## What should you compare?

Evaluate:

- Product and catalog complexity.
- Checkout and payment requirements.
- Inventory, fulfillment, and returns.
- International selling and localization.
- ERP, CRM, PIM, and marketplace integrations.
- Performance and experience control.
- Security and compliance responsibility.
- Internal skills and maintenance capacity.
- Three-to-five-year ownership cost.
- Data portability and platform risk.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Shopify only for small businesses?

No. It serves organizations of different sizes, but suitability depends on required capabilities and operating model.

### Is custom ecommerce always more scalable?

No. Scalability depends on architecture and operations. Managed platforms can remove major infrastructure burdens.

### Can we start on Shopify and move later?

Yes, but plan clean product data, integrations, URLs, and analytics to reduce future migration cost.
