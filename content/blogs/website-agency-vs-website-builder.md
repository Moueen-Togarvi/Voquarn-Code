Choose a **website builder** for a simple site you can plan and maintain yourself. Choose a **website agency** when strategy, custom design, integrations, migration, or conversion performance justify specialist help.

## When is a website builder enough?

A builder is a practical choice when:

- The website is small and informational.
- A standard template fits the brand.
- There are few integrations.
- Someone can prepare content and manage updates.
- Speed and low initial cost are the priority.

Review subscription tiers, transaction fees, export options, template limits, and add-on costs before committing.

## When does an agency add value?

An agency can coordinate audience research, information architecture, copy, UX, visual design, development, analytics, SEO migration, testing, and launch. This matters when the website supports a significant sales process or complex operation.

An agency should not add complexity for its own sake. It should explain why each custom choice improves an outcome.

## How does total cost differ?

Builders reduce initial production cost but require your time and may create subscription or migration costs. Agencies require more upfront investment but can reduce internal workload and address problems that templates do not solve.

Compare ownership, ongoing support, and the likely cost of changing platforms later.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can an agency use a website builder?

Yes. A capable agency should recommend the simplest platform that meets the actual requirements.

### Is custom code always faster?

No. Custom code offers control, but speed depends on architecture, content, media, and engineering quality.

### Can a builder site rank in search?

Yes. Search performance depends on technical accessibility, useful content, authority, and user experience as well as the platform.
