Choose **Shopify** for a managed commerce environment with less infrastructure responsibility. Choose **WooCommerce** when WordPress content flexibility, hosting control, or open-source customization is central and you can maintain the stack responsibly.

## What is the core difference?

Shopify is a hosted commerce platform. The vendor manages much of the underlying infrastructure and provides a coordinated admin and ecosystem. WooCommerce is an ecommerce plugin for WordPress; you choose hosting, implementation, extensions, and maintenance arrangements.

## Which is easier to operate?

Shopify generally reduces technical administration. WooCommerce can be straightforward with a good host and disciplined plugin selection, but the site owner or partner remains responsible for more updates, compatibility, backups, and security decisions.

## Which offers more control?

WooCommerce provides broad code and hosting control. Shopify offers strong customization within its platform and extension model, but some areas are intentionally controlled. More control is useful only when the team can govern it.

## How should cost be compared?

Include plan or hosting fees, themes, apps or plugins, payment costs, development, maintenance, monitoring, security, and internal operations. A low entry price can grow as extension needs increase on either platform.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which is better for content marketing?

WordPress has a mature publishing ecosystem. Shopify also supports content, and the best choice depends on editorial complexity and integration needs.

### Which platform is more secure?

Both can be operated securely. Shopify manages more platform security centrally; WooCommerce security depends more heavily on hosting, updates, extensions, and administration.

### Can both support international stores?

Yes, with capability varying by setup, region, currency, language, tax, and fulfillment needs. Validate the exact markets before choosing.
