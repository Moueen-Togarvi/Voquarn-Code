### collaboration

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Generate an original realistic editorial photograph for the About page of a Pakistan-based web development agency. Three adult South Asian software professionals collaborating naturally around a wooden desk in a bright modern studio, one woman and two men in smart casual everyday clothing, looking at laptop screens with code and responsive website layouts. Candid working moment, no one looking at the camera, natural faces and anatomically correct hands, plausible real office details, warm daylight from large windows, subtle orange stationery. Portrait 4:5 framing, people and laptops central and crop-safe for a rounded website collage tile. Style like authentic documentary workplace photography with a 35mm camera lens, natural skin and fabric texture, not a 3D render, no floating UI panels, no neon sci-fi effects, no visible logos or watermark. These are fictional professionals in a representative agency scene, not portraits of specific company employees.
