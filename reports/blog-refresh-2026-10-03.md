## Keyword evidence and limitations

The full inventory is in `blog-keyword-map-2026-10-03.json`, including a primary keyword, secondary variants, category, and indexing policy for every article. It is an editorial intent map, **not a keyword-volume dataset**.

No connected Search Console or quantitative keyword-research account was available for this run. Semrush and GSC Wizard were suggested, but installation/connection was not confirmed. Search volume, difficulty, CPC, current ranking, and “highest-volume” status are therefore **not claimed**. These values must be obtained from a dated source with an explicit country/database before using them to rank opportunities quantitatively.

The six new topics were chosen because they answer distinct, commercially relevant decisions connected to Voquarn's services. Priority below reflects editorial relevance, not measured popularity. Google [does not use the keywords meta tag for ranking](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/special-tags); field cleanup alone is not a ranking result. The substantive article content, relevance, supported claims, and usable discovery are the meaningful deliverables.
