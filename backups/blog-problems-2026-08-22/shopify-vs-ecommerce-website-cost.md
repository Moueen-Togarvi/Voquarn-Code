In a **Shopify vs ecommerce website cost** comparison, Shopify commonly lowers infrastructure and standard-feature effort, while custom ecommerce requires more engineering but may reduce expensive workarounds for unusual operations. Compare total ownership over several years, not launch quotes alone.

## Which costs belong in the model?

Include:

- Discovery, design, and storefront build.
- Platform plan or cloud infrastructure.
- Themes, apps, extensions, and licenses.
- Payment and transaction costs.
- Catalog and customer data migration.
- ERP, CRM, fulfillment, and marketing integrations.
- Testing, security, monitoring, and backups.
- Maintenance and support.
- Internal content and store operations.
- Future change and migration cost.

Use realistic order volume, regions, staff time, and feature growth when modeling variable costs.

## Why can Shopify cost grow?

Advanced plans, multiple apps, custom integrations, international requirements, and specialized storefront work increase cost. Apps may also overlap or create ongoing operational dependencies.

## Why can custom ecommerce cost grow?

The owner funds architecture, infrastructure, security, feature development, compatibility, and maintenance. Underestimating ongoing engineering is a common budgeting error.

## How should the decision be made?

Create a three-year scenario for expected growth and a downside scenario. Price the must-have workflows on both options, then compare commercial fit, risk, speed, and internal capacity. Choose the option with the strongest total business case, not the smallest first invoice.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Shopify pricing predictable?

Core plan pricing is visible, but total cost depends on apps, payments, development, and operations. Confirm current terms directly with the platform.

### Is custom ecommerce a one-time cost?

No. Every live platform needs hosting, monitoring, updates, support, and improvement.

### Which option launches faster?

Shopify often does for standard stores. Complex migration and integrations can narrow the difference.
