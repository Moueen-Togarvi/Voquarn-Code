A **software product company** sells a repeatable product to many customers, while a software service company designs, builds, or operates solutions for specific clients. Choose a product for a common need and a service partner when your workflow, integration, or competitive requirement is meaningfully different.

## How does ownership differ?

With a product, the vendor normally owns the core platform and controls its roadmap. You receive usage rights under a subscription or license. With custom services, the contract can transfer ownership of project code and designs to you, although third-party and pre-existing components remain separately licensed.

## Which option is faster?

An established product can be configured much faster than custom software. The advantage narrows when extensive workarounds, integrations, or process changes are required. A service company takes longer initially but can shape the solution around the operating model.

## How should cost be compared?

Compare total cost over several years:

- Subscription or license fees.
- Setup, configuration, and training.
- Integration and migration.
- Custom development.
- Internal administration.
- Support and change costs.
- Switching and data-exit costs.

Custom software requires more upfront investment and ongoing ownership. Products spread development cost across many customers.

## Can you combine both models?

Yes. Many effective systems use established products for standard capabilities and custom software for differentiating workflows. Confirm API access, data portability, usage limits, and vendor roadmap risk before integrating deeply.

## Frequently asked questions

### When is a software product the better choice?

When the process is common, the product fits most requirements, and speed matters more than unique control.

### When is a service company the better choice?

When the workflow creates competitive value, several systems must be connected, or product limitations create material operating cost.

### Who maintains custom software?

Your internal team or a contracted service partner. Maintenance ownership should be agreed before launch.
