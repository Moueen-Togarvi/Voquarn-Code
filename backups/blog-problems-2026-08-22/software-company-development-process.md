A dependable **software company development process** moves from problem validation to small, testable releases and ongoing learning. The exact framework can vary, but responsibilities, evidence, quality controls, and decisions should always be visible.

## What happens during discovery?

The team studies users, workflows, goals, constraints, existing systems, and risks. It then defines measurable outcomes, the smallest useful scope, and unanswered questions. Discovery may include interviews, analytics review, prototypes, data assessment, or technical experiments.

## How do design and engineering work together?

Design turns priority journeys into flows and interfaces. Engineering tests feasibility, data needs, permissions, integrations, and operational behavior. Working together early prevents attractive designs that are expensive or unsafe to implement.

## What happens during development?

Work is divided into small increments with clear acceptance conditions. Engineers use version control, peer review, automated checks, and separate environments. Stakeholders review working software regularly instead of waiting for a final reveal.

## How is quality verified?

Quality assurance covers more than clicking the happy path. Testing should consider errors, permissions, devices, accessibility, performance, security, integrations, and data integrity. The depth depends on the consequences of failure.

## What happens at launch?

A launch plan identifies data migration, configuration, domains, certificates, monitoring, backups, rollback, communication, and support owners. Teams should confirm how success will be measured after real users arrive.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is agile the only good process?

No. The label matters less than short feedback loops, visible priorities, working increments, and responsible change control.

### When should documentation be written?

Throughout delivery. Keep decision records, setup guidance, architecture context, and operating procedures current enough for another capable person to continue.

### Does development end at launch?

No. Live software needs observation, maintenance, security updates, user feedback, and planned improvement.
