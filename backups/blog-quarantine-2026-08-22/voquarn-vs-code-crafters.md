## What should you compare?

Use the same scorecard for both candidates:

- Fit for the required website, software, ecommerce, SEO, or automation work.
- Relevant live projects and the team’s exact contribution.
- Assigned people, seniority, and availability.
- Discovery, design, engineering, testing, and launch process.
- Security, hosting, source code, and account ownership.
- Timeline assumptions, exclusions, and change control.
- Support, documentation, and handover.
- Total cost for the stated scope.
