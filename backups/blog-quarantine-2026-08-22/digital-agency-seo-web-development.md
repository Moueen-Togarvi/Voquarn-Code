A **digital agency should connect SEO and web development** because search visibility depends on content and technical implementation working together. Separating them can lead to designs that hide valuable information or SEO recommendations that the platform cannot maintain.

## Where should the disciplines collaborate?

They should work together on:

- Audience and search-intent research.
- Site structure and internal linking.
- Reusable page templates.
- Crawlability and index controls.
- Performance and mobile experience.
- Canonicals, redirects, and migrations.
- Structured data eligibility.
- Content publishing and governance.
- Analytics and search monitoring.

SEO review should happen during planning and design, not only before launch.

## What does the development team own?

Developers implement semantic markup, rendering, navigation, metadata systems, sitemaps, robots rules, performance controls, redirects, and structured data. They also make content features usable for editors so search quality can be sustained.

## What does the SEO team own?

SEO specialists connect customer demand to content, inspect crawl and index behavior, define migration requirements, review templates, and measure organic outcomes. They should prioritize recommendations by impact and engineering effort.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does a fast website automatically rank higher?

No. Speed supports user experience and technical quality, but relevance, usefulness, competition, links, and many other factors matter.

### Is structured data guaranteed to create rich results?

No. It must be accurate and eligible, and search engines decide whether to display enhancements.

### Who owns SEO after launch?

Assign an ongoing owner for monitoring, content, technical changes, and experiments. Launch is the beginning of operational search work.
