**Software agency cost** depends mainly on scope uncertainty, product complexity, team composition, quality requirements, and the level of post-launch support. A credible estimate connects money to assumptions and deliverables instead of offering one unexplained number.

## What changes the price most?

The largest cost drivers are usually:

- Number and complexity of user journeys.
- Custom design and interaction requirements.
- Third-party integrations and data migration.
- User roles, permissions, payments, or subscriptions.
- Security, compliance, performance, and availability needs.
- Browser, device, and accessibility coverage.
- Documentation, training, maintenance, and support.

A five-page informational site and a customer portal may look similar in a feature list but require very different engineering effort.

## Which pricing model should you use?

A fixed price fits stable, testable scope. Time-and-materials fits evolving products where learning changes priorities. A hybrid approach often funds discovery at a fixed price, then delivers approved increments within a controlled monthly budget.

Ask the agency to show what is included, what is excluded, and how changes are priced. Also request a confidence range when important questions remain unanswered.

## What costs are commonly forgotten?

Budget for discovery, content preparation, analytics, hosting, software subscriptions, payment fees, data cleanup, launch support, and ongoing maintenance. Your internal team will also spend time reviewing designs, providing content, testing workflows, and making decisions.

## How can you control the budget?

Rank requirements by business outcome, release the smallest useful version, and review working software frequently. Maintain one decision owner and a visible list of assumptions and risks. Postponing low-value features is safer than quietly reducing testing or security.

## Frequently asked questions

### Should I ask several agencies for estimates?

Yes. Give each agency the same brief and compare scope, assumptions, team, evidence, and support—not price alone.

### How much contingency should a software project have?

The right contingency depends on uncertainty. Legacy integrations, incomplete requirements, or unverified data deserve more reserve than a familiar, bounded build.

### Is the cheapest agency the highest risk?

Not automatically. Risk rises when the estimate cannot explain how the promised outcome will be delivered.
