Choose a **full-service digital agency** when several connected channels need one accountable plan. Choose a specialist when one high-value problem demands deep expertise and your team can coordinate the surrounding work.

## What does full-service mean?

It may include strategy, brand, design, web development, SEO, content, paid media, social, email, analytics, and automation. Because definitions vary, confirm which capabilities are truly in-house and which are subcontracted.

## What are the tradeoffs?

A full-service team can reduce handoffs, keep messaging consistent, and connect data across channels. A specialist may bring deeper experience, faster diagnosis, and more focused methods in a narrow area.

Neither structure guarantees quality. A broad agency may be shallow in a critical discipline, while a specialist may optimize one channel without seeing its effect on the wider customer journey.

## How should you decide?

Map the outcome, channels involved, internal skills, coordination capacity, and dominant risk. If three or more disciplines must change together, integration can be valuable. If one bottleneck is well understood, specialist help may be more efficient.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can a full-service agency use external experts?

Yes. Ask who they are, how quality is managed, and whether the commercial relationship is transparent.

### Is one agency easier to manage?

Usually, but centralized responsibility can create dependency. Keep access to accounts, data, creative assets, and documentation.

### Can we start with a specialist and expand later?

Yes. Define interfaces and measurement from the start so other partners can join without rebuilding the foundation.
